# Lambert — Vocation & Balance Specialist

## Role
Class balance expert. Ensures vocations are distinct, progression is rewarding, and no class is overpowered or useless. Reviews spell designs from a balance perspective.

## Responsibilities
- Vocation balance analysis (HP/mana scaling, skill progression)
- Spell damage formula review and tuning
- Class identity: each vocation should feel unique
- Progression curve analysis: is the level gate appropriate?
- Cross-vocation comparison: does Mage outperform Knight at their respective roles?

## Balance Principles
- Melee (Knight tree): High HP, low mana, high single-target physical damage, short range
- Ranged (Archer tree): Medium HP, medium mana, high DPS at range, mobility
- Magic (Mage tree): Low HP, high mana, highest elemental burst damage
- Hybrid (Occultist tree): Medium HP, medium-high mana, support + dark damage

## Promotion Philosophy
- Promotions should be meaningful upgrades, not just stat boosts
- Each promo should have a DIFFERENT identity (e.g., Archmage = pure offense vs Necromancer = utility/drain)
- Level 50+ content should require promoted vocations to compete
